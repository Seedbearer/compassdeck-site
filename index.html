Questions

Before you ask

Do I need a paid Notion plan?

No. Everything in CompassDeck runs on Notion's free plan. A paid plan only matters once you're sharing portals with a very large number of guests at once.

Can my clients see my notes on other clients?

No. The client portal is a separate page you duplicate and share per client — it never links back into your CRM or any other client's data.

What if I coach outside career/leadership specifically?

The structure — CRM, sessions, homework, portal — works for any 1:1 coaching practice. The values/strengths/skills assessment is written for career coaching, but every field is plain text you can relabel in minutes.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time template purchase. You duplicate it for every client you take on, with no per-seat or per-client cost.
